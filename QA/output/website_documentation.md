## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the **CRM Pro Platform** user guide. This document provides a comprehensive overview of the platform, its features, and step-by-step instructions on how to use them. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced user, this guide will help you navigate and utilize the platform effectively.

---

## 2. Website Overview
The **CRM Pro Platform** is a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) tool designed to showcase autonomous testing capabilities using QA.tech. It provides a centralized interface for managing customers, companies, and deals, making it an ideal solution for businesses looking to streamline their operations.

---

## 3. Features
### Dashboard
- **Purpose**: Provides an overview of the platform's key metrics.
- **Details**:
  - Displays the total number of customers.
  - Shows recent customer activity (currently unavailable due to a database connection issue).
  - Navigation links to other sections: Customers, Companies, and Deals.

### Customers
- **Purpose**: Manage customer information.
- **Details**:
  - Lists customer details such as name, contact information, company, and address.
  - Includes an **Add Customer** button for adding new customers.

### Companies
- **Purpose**: Manage company information.
- **Details**:
  - Lists companies with details such as industry, founding year, and a brief description.
  - Example companies include Cyberdyne Systems, Stark Industries, and Aperture Science.

### Deals
- **Purpose**: Track and manage business deals.
- **Details**:
  - Categorizes deals into stages: NEW, TALKING, MEETING, PROPOSAL, and CLOSED - WON.
  - Lists deal details such as title, company, and value.
  - Example deals include 'Enterprise License' with TechCorp for $50,000 and 'AI Implementation' with SmartSystems for $150,000.

---

## 4. How to Use Each Feature
### Dashboard
1. Navigate to the Dashboard by clicking the **Dashboard** link in the navigation menu.
2. View the total number of customers.
3. Check recent customer activity (note: currently unavailable).

### Customers
1. Navigate to the Customers page by clicking the **Customers** link in the navigation menu.
2. View the list of customers and their details.
3. To add a new customer, click the **Add Customer** button and fill in the required information.

### Companies
1. Navigate to the Companies page by clicking the **Companies** link in the navigation menu.
2. View the list of companies and their details.

### Deals
1. Navigate to the Deals page by clicking the **Deals** link in the navigation menu.
2. View deals categorized by stages.
3. Review deal details such as title, company, and value.

---

## 5. Feature Dependencies
- The **Dashboard** serves as the central hub, providing links to all other sections.
- The **Customers** and **Companies** sections are independent but may share data (e.g., a customer may belong to a company).
- The **Deals** section depends on data from both the **Customers** and **Companies** sections to associate deals with specific entities.
